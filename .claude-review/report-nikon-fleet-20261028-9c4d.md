On real scales it exceeds one step near the top: WBTune\* (0..1224, 1/step) accepts an adjacent step at ≥ ~1000 (about 225 of 1225 positions, probed); WBTuneColorTempEx (2500..10000, 10 K/step) accepts 10000 vs 9990 exactly at the limit; MFDriveStep tolerates ~30 steps at the top.
